# Checkpoint 17 bounded navigation ledger

Intended IDs are additive `Confectory.BaseUI::{RoundedBox,RoundedHit,RoundedButton,RoundedInput,AnchorPanel,ClipRegions}` and owning `*Body` providers. Public contracts are bounded rectangle rasterization/hit testing, shared Button decoration/routing, anchored placement and opaque clip subtraction; no editor/project/table model semantics. `Example.RoundedGame::Main` consumes them without authoring, Window or AI dependencies. Existing Button/control identity, capture, event deduplication and lifetime remain authoritative.

`RoundedBox(bounds,radius,RGB)` emits merged horizontal rectangle bands matching pixel-center `RoundedHit`. `RoundedButton` delegates to Button and replaces presentation only. `RoundedInput` applies matching rounded hit geometry before ButtonInput; keyboard and cancellation remain shared. Radius clamps to half the smaller dimension. `AnchorPanel(viewport,anchor,size,gap,direction)` returns xywh/direction, prefers requested direction, flips to opposite if it fits, otherwise chooses greatest available area in deterministic order and clamps. Oversized panels may overlap their anchor rather than leave the viewport. `ClipRegions(clip,masks)` subtracts opaque bounding rectangles without overlapping fragments. Rounded panel corners conservatively reserve their bounding region for underlying text. Budgets remain explicit, without per-raster-band hit records.
